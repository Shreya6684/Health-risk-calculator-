# 🩺 Health Risk Calculator

Welcome to the **Health Risk Calculator**! 👋 

This is an interactive C++ command-line tool designed to estimate overall health risks based on vital biometric markers and daily lifestyle habits. It calculates your Body Mass Index (BMI) and runs a simple diagnostic risk-assessment algorithm covering cardiovascular, diabetic, and physical wellness parameters.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **BMI Calculation:** Computes standard Body Mass Index from height (m) and weight (kg).
* **Multi-Factor Risk Assessment:** Evaluates risk points across multiple indicators:
  * **Weight Status:** Flags underweight or overweight ranges.
  * **Cardiovascular Risk:** Checks for high blood pressure (>140 mmHg), smoking habits, and regular physical activity.
  * **Diabetes Risk:** Evaluates fasting glucose levels (Normal, Pre-diabetic, Diabetic ranges).
  * **Age Factors:** Considers age-related wellness thresholds (>50 years).
* **Instant Health Status Categorization:** Classifies overall health into **Low Risk ✅**, **Moderate Risk ⚠️**, or **High Risk ❌** based on a cumulative score out of 12.
